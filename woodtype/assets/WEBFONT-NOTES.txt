Shuimu Silk and Shuimu Old Song are renamed web subsets of the bundled licensed fonts. Glyph outlines and advance widths are retained. Full original fonts remain at SourceHanSerifSC-Light.otf and I.Ming-8.10.ttf. To restore the original rendering, replace each core WOFF2 src in shuimu-silk.css/shuimu-oldsong.css with the corresponding original font file, remove unicode-range from that first rule, and remove the second rule. License files are included unchanged.
